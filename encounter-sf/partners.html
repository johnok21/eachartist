About us

Led by artists for artists.

A coalition of community arts and culture organizations is the operating system that brings this map to life.

Encounter SF connects neighborhood arts hubs into one network, with a shared calendar, a citywide map, a cultural transit pass, and an annual festival. Partners share permitting, insurance, and finance, and decide with artists and residents where new hubs open.

SOMArts

Lead organization. A civic cultural center uplifting community artists for nearly fifty years.

Each Artist Cultural Hub

Leads community engagement and builds the digital platform behind the pass.

Art + Water

Opening at Pier 29 with free studios, tuition-free instruction, and free programs for children and families.

Ruth Williams Bayview Opera House

Anchors performance and culture in Bayview-Hunters Point.

SF Art Week

Produces a citywide map and festival celebrating arts in San Francisco.

Root Division

25 years as a sustainable arts hub serving visual artists.

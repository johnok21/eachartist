"Nobody's Coming to Save SF Arts" — The Scene Must Save Itself

Five prominent galleries closed in Q4 2025. CCA announced it will cease operations after 2026-27 and sold both campuses to Vanderbilt. Mayor Lurie celebrated the Vanderbilt deal on Instagram — seated in front of a painting by a CCA alum. Max Blue's column lays out the full picture: public funding is shrinking, private capital is flowing to initiatives that don't support the community, and the art scene is on track to become culturally anemic by 2027 unless the people who make it step up.

This is the context we're all working in. Read it.

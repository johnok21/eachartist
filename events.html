Artist Meetups

Where we gather, nourish, and share

Art-making can sometimes be lonely, and the world rarely gives us room to simply be creative people together. Artist Meetups are a recurring, reliable place where Bay Area artists get together to play, to workshop what we're making, to ask for help and offer it, to rest, and to celebrate our contributions to the community. These are spaces for tending, for keeping our inner creative alive and well, and for refilling the well when it runs dry. Our meetups are free, collaborative, and meant to be shared.

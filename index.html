Artistic & Economic Freedom

EACH is a community-artist-led organization piloting solutions at the intersection of the arts and community health — addressing loneliness and social isolation, strengthening belonging and mental wellbeing, and building a solidarity economy that gives Bay Area artists a sustainable livelihood.

Artists are the imaginary and nervous systems of our communities. Art is how we process together — how we grieve, celebrate, question, and imagine what's next. In the Bay Area, too many artists are isolated, burned out, and getting pushed out. And too many neighbors are disconnected from the art and artists who could help them make sense of what they're moving through.

EACH exists because artists — and the communities that ground them — deserve connective tissue.

The Bay Needs Its Artists

Imagine a Bay Area where garage doors roll up after dark, where sidewalks and backyards and storefronts open to artists sharing their work. Neighbors stopping to listen, to look, to feel something together. A more connected, awe-inspired Bay — because when artists are resourced and connected, and when neighbors can find their way to the artists who see them, there is more space for truth and play in public life. More beauty. More courage. More civic imagination.

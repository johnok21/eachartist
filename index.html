EACH ARTIST

A cooperative hub by-and-for Bay Area artists to practice mutual care, shared economics, and unabashed creativity.

eachartist.org

Artistic & Economic Freedom

Artists are the immune and imaginary systems of our communities. In the Bay Area, too many of us are isolated, burned out, and getting pushed out. EACH exists because artists deserve connective tissue — relationships, resource-sharing, and healing so we can stay rooted in our work and our freedom.

We Gather

Small groups of artists meeting consistently to exchange ideas, opportunities, care, and feedback.

We Make

Art resonant with our home — shaped by the realities and possibilities of our local community and land.

We Share

Resources, tools, space, connections, and money via mutual aid. Pop-ups, shows, art walks, teach-ins, and more.

We Invest

Community contributions — tax-deductible donations, sliding-scale tickets — flow into a shared pool managed by advisory circles of neighborhood artists who receive financial coaching and shape creative budgets together.

Culture Circles

Our pilot program: small groups of local artists who meet regularly to build shared economic and community power over time. EACH coaches circle-keepers, connects circles to grants and fellowships, distributes small circulating funds, and handles the admin so you can focus on your art.

San Francisco Needs Its Artists

Imagine an artistic renaissance — garage doors rolling up after dark, sidewalks and backyards and balconies and storefronts opening to a wave of artists ready to share their work. Art pouring into the streets. Neighbors stopping to listen, to look, to feel something together. A more connected, awe-inspired city — because when artists are resourced and rooted, there is more space for truth and play in public life. More beauty. More courage. More civic imagination.

Gather Together

We are excited to launch into planning our first community town hall in the spring of 2026 — a space that welcomes artists across generations, mediums, and walks of life into dialogue about the future of arts in our city.

No panelists. No consultants. This one is for you.

We want to create a session where artists can show up with their lived experience, their questions, their hopes, and their honest read of what this city needs. No performance of expertise required. No polished talking points. Just authentic voice, shared imagination, and real conversation.

This first town hall — and the gatherings that follow — will help shape our path forward as a community. We are treating this page as a placeholder while we build the format, invite people in, and make sure the room reflects the values we want to practice together.

What we're building toward

A multigenerational, cross-medium gathering rooted in care, cultural power, and the future of artists in San Francisco.

Across generations

Emerging artists, elders, culture workers, and neighbors in one room — learning from one another rather than sorting by status.

Across mediums

Visual artists, musicians, writers, dancers, filmmakers, designers, organizers, and the forms that do not fit neat categories.

Across walks of life

Artists with different relationships to institutions, money, housing, family, labor, and place — all bringing essential perspective.

Programming details are still taking shape. In the meantime, you can reach out through the contact page or join the mailing list there to hear when the first gathering is announced.
